WHO I AM

About Me

First-year games development student at York St John University. I make games, build 3D models, and am looking for an internship.

Introduction

I got into games development because I enjoyed taking games apart in my head — figuring out why certain spaces feel tense, or how a game gets you to care about something without directly telling you to. That side of it still interests me the most: the design decisions behind how an experience actually feels.

I'm in my first year at York St John University studying BSc Computer Science with Game Development. Before that I did a Level 3 Games Development course at York College, where I got two distinctions. I'm looking for an internship where I can get some real experience and learn how a studio actually works.

My Story

After my GCSEs I had no real plan. I went to a college open day, ended up in a 3D modelling workshop, and found it genuinely interesting. My tutor Steve was a big part of that — he gave me a realistic picture of what games development actually involves, which made me want to pursue it properly.

I finished with two distinctions and moved on to university. That's pretty much it — no grand narrative, just found something I was good at and enjoyed, and kept going.
