Ancient Greece — Acropolis Project

A large-scale historical reconstruction project modelling the Acropolis of Athens in Blender, including the Parthenon, surrounding structures, columns with PBR marble and gold textures, a walled citadel, and a low-poly Trojan Horse prop.

Blender Substance Painter Architecture Modelling PBR Texturing Historical Reference

Full Acropolis — Parthenon and all surrounding structures placed on the hill platform.

Parthenon — Isolated temple model with full colonnaded exterior, pediments, and stepped base.

Blueprint reference — Columns placed over a historical Parthenon plan — 8x17 layout built to measured scale.

Column capital wireframe — Abacus and echinus detail with clean edge loop topology.

Textured columns — White marble with gold band rings and a sandy base plinth — PBR in Substance Painter.

Walled citadel — Circular fortification with towers, battlements, and a columned temple structure at the centre.

Trojan Horse — Low-poly stylised prop with wood grain texture and dark iron hoof blocks.

Column & staircase — Full-height fluted column with a spiral staircase wrapping the circular base.

Citadel (alt view) — Different angle showing the gate entrance, semicircular wall, and interior temple columns.

Textured wall section — White marble walls with dark stone trim capping and a single column prop — full PBR pass.
