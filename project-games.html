Solo Game · College Final Major Project
Astros Visit

A first-person exploration game set in 2099. Earth has been abandoned following an irreversible nuclear disaster — the human race has moved to Mars as a temporary measure. You play as an astronaut returning to Earth to assess whether the planet is habitable again. The game takes place inside an overgrown, post-apocalyptic power plant, with the player navigating decaying industrial environments reclaimed by nature.

Interactive Educational Series · College Project
Ancient Greece Interactive Educational Series

A fully rendered interactive educational game series set in Ancient Greece. Covers key historical settings including the Acropolis, the Circus Maximus chariot racing arena, and the Trojan Horse. Assets were modelled in Blender, textured with PBR materials, and brought into Unity for gameplay. The project involved full environment design, character placement, and interactive scene building.

Chariot lineup — Charioteers standing in their coloured chariots at the stone arch starting gates.

Commission Work · Unity ML-Agents
AI Emotion Adoption — ML-Agents Commission

Commission work for a Unity ML-Agents deep learning research project exploring how AI agents can adopt and express emotions. My role involved creating the agent character models in Blender — small blob-like creatures rendered in different colours to represent different emotional states. The models were built to be simple enough for real-time use in a reinforcement learning environment while still having a clear visual identity per emotion. Also included a concept aircraft render produced as part of the same commission.
